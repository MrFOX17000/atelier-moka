## Fichiers générés et prompts finaux

### Scène du café

Chemin final : `public/images/hero-moka.jpg`. Utilisée dans le hero et la galerie.

Prompt :

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: website hero photograph for Atelier Moka, a premium warm ceramic-painting café in Aix-en-Provence. Primary request: editorial close-up of a cream ceramic coffee mug with hand-painted olive-green botanical leaves on a rustic oak table, a small saucer of coffee and a ceramic palette with terracotta and sage glazes beside it; a person's natural hand holds a fine paintbrush near the mug. Background: softly blurred welcoming Provençal café, beige limewash walls, wooden furniture, shelving with ceramics, a few olive branches. Composition: landscape 3:2, mug clearly featured in lower central-right area, intimate authentic lifestyle photography. Lighting: warm natural late afternoon light from a window, gentle realistic shadows. Palette: cream, coffee brown, clay terracotta, pale sage. Materials: tactile ceramic glaze, worn oak, linen. No text, logos, watermarks, UI or collages. Beautiful but believable photography.
